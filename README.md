# CenturyLink-Churn-Retain-Revenue-Analysis-


A Telecommunication Churn, Retain, and Revenue Analysis evaluates subscriber behavior to measure how many customers leave, how many stay, and the financial impact on the business.

# Churn Risk Concentration

- Churn is not evenly distributed across the customer base; contract type, tenure, age group, and internet service are key segmentation dimensions.
  
- Customers in shorter-tenure segments should receive stronger onboarding and early-retention interventions.
  
- Contract-level churn differences can help identify where flexible or short-term customers require targeted retention offers.

# Customer Retention


- Retention should be monitored by both customer tenure and contract type, rather than using an overall retention rate alone.
  
- Existing/longer-tenure customers represent an important opportunity for cross-sell, upgrades, and loyalty initiatives.
  
- New customers with early signs of churn can be prioritized for proactive engagement before they become lost customers.

# Revenue Impact

-Customer churn has a direct financial impact through revenue lost and reduced profit contribution.

-Revenue loss should be analyzed by tenure band to identify the customer segments creating the largest financial exposure.

-Monthly charge and customer status can help identify whether higher-value customers are disproportionately represented among churned customers.

# • Target Attrition Clustered Pools: Focus resources on isolating where the combination of high operational costs and high customer turnover overlaps. If paper-billed internet accounts exhibit a spike in turnover, launch automated migration campaigns to move those users to paperless environments.

# • Product Pipeline Volume: Watch the onboarding pipeline closely to see if phone-only additions are flattening out. If new customers heavily favor high-tier internet, it justifies sundown strategies for legacy voice operations.

# • Net Margin Maintenance: Do not look at historical gross revenue in isolation. Ensure the Profit Summary page strictly evaluates how Total Refunds and active operational costs erode your cash position month-over-month.
